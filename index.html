Prynix

Prynix is a team of high school students who share a common passion for technology and innovation. We believe that age is just a number when it comes to technological innovations.

Founded and led by Prasoon Kandel, our community brings together young minds from around the world to collaborate, learn, and build the future together.

Hack Club Affiliation

Prynix is proudly affiliated with the Hack Club network, a global community of high school coding clubs and makers. This affiliation provides our members with access to resources, a worldwide network of student developers, and opportunities to participate in hackathons and collaborative projects.
